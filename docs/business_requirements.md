# QuickCommerceIQ — Business Requirements & Problem Statement

## Context
A quick-commerce operator running **25 dark stores** across **Bengaluru (7), Mumbai (7), Delhi NCR (6), and Chennai (5)** experienced **~28% quarter-on-quarter order growth** over May–October 2026, yet **order-level Net Contribution Margin (NCM) deteriorated from ~₹18/order to negative territory (-₹3 to -₹6/order)**.

## Core Leadership Questions Addressed
1. **Which dark stores are losing money?** (Distinguishing order-level contribution margin from fixed-cost-inclusive store operating profit.)
2. **Which hourly periods have the highest SLA breaches (>12 minutes)?**
3. **Are delays driven by picking, rider assignment, or transit?**
4. **How much GMV and margin are lost due to SKU stockouts during 7 PM–10 PM IST?**
5. **How severely does heavy rain inflate rider payouts and SLA breaches?**
6. **How does first-order delivery speed (Fast <12m vs Medium 12–20m vs Slow >20m) affect Month 0–6 retention?**
7. **Does raising the free-delivery threshold from ₹199 to ₹249 improve Net Profit per Randomized User (NPPU) without violating retention or abandonment guardrails?**
8. **Which operational interventions should leadership prioritize to restore profitability?**

> **Synthetic Data Disclaimer:** All records are reproducibly generated (`seed=42`) to simulate realistic quick-commerce operations and must not be represented as actual third-party company financials.
